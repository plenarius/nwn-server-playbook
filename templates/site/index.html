My World

A persistent world for Neverwinter Nights: Enhanced Edition.

How to join

  1. Start Neverwinter Nights: Enhanced Edition.
  2. Choose Multiplayer, then Join Internet Game.
  3. Choose Direct Connect and enter game.example.org:5121
  4. The game downloads what it needs by itself. Make a character and step in.
